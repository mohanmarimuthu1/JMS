# How to make a bill

Print this page and keep it by the computer.

## Making an Invoice (a bill with GST)

1. Open the billing website. Sign in with the shop email and password.
2. Click **New Invoice**.
3. Type the customer's name. If they've been billed before, a list
   will drop down — click their name. If they're new, just keep
   typing and fill in their address and GSTIN (if they have one).
4. Check the date. It's already set to today.
5. For each item: type what it is, the HSN code (if you know it), how
   many, and the rate. Click **+ Add line** for more items.
6. When everything looks right, click **Save & Print**.
7. The bill number is given automatically — you'll see it on the
   printed page. You never need to type it yourself.
8. The print screen opens. Click **Print / Save PDF** and print it as
   usual.

## Making a Delivery Challan (no GST, for sending goods)

Same as above, but click **New Delivery Challan** instead. Pick a
**Purpose** (Job work, Sale on approval, etc.) instead of typing a
rate — a delivery challan has no price or GST on it.

## Finding an old bill

1. Click **History** on the home screen.
2. Type the customer's name, or the bill number, in the search box.
3. Click **Print** next to the bill to print it again.

## If you made a mistake on a bill

**Do not delete it.** Bills are numbered for tax purposes — deleting
one leaves a number missing with no explanation, which is not
allowed. Instead:

1. Find the bill in **History**.
2. Click **Cancel**, type a short reason (e.g. "wrong quantity,
   redone as bill 215"), and click **Confirm**.
3. Make a new, correct bill.

A cancelled bill stays in the list with a line through it and prints
with a red **CANCELLED** stamp across it, so it can never be mistaken
for a real bill later.

## If something goes wrong

- **Page won't load / can't sign in** — check the internet connection
  first. Nothing you typed is lost; go back to the same screen once
  it's back and continue.
- **Typed a bill and the internet dropped before saving** — reopen
  the page. What you typed should still be there; just click
  **Save & Print** again.
- **Not sure if a bill actually saved** — check **History**. If it's
  not there, it wasn't saved, and nothing was charged against a bill
  number — it's safe to just make it again.
- **Anything else** — call [your number here].
